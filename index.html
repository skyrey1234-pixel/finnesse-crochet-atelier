THE ARCHIVE NARRATIVE · SIX CHAPTERS

FROM ONE HANDMADE HAT
TO AN ENTIRE WORLD.

Every folder in this archive tells a chapter of the journey—from Farah’s original dusty-rose prototype selfie through 20 signature headwear icons and 38 runway ensembles, to the newest full-apparel drop and the made-to-order Atelier Couture tier. Click any chapter to filter the catalog.

CHAPTER 01 · THE ORIGIN

Farah’s First Stitch & Cherry Drop

It started with a chunky, ribbed dusty-coral crochet beanie handmade by Farah. Because of the flat top-seam construction, the two corners naturally rose into playful cat ears—giving birth to the signature FINNESSE silhouette and the original Cherry Crush look.

CHAPTER 02 · STREET & SPIRIT

Faith, Fire & The SKY Cyber Lab

Expanding the silhouette into graphic intarsia: "THE WAY" Faith × Streetwear cross and crown series, Midnight Web gothic embroidery, Violet Venom flames, and the 20-piece SKY / CODE / 404 / LOFI / COSMOS studio lab series.

CHAPTER 03 · HOMETOWN DYNASTY

904 in the Blood: Duval Jaguar

Jacksonville (30.3322° N, 81.6557° W) turned up to 100. Custom teal, obsidian, and bullion-gold jaguar rosette stitch patterns, fierce woven jaguar-head emblems, game-day crop-and-short trios, and the 6-Piece Duval Collector Capsule.

CHAPTER 04 · HIGH-FASHION RUNWAY

Crochet Couture & Resort Sets

Taking handmade crochet to the runway: 6-piece gauntlet and leg-warmer skirt sets, cat-hooded pullovers and joggers, open duster cardigans, square-neck maxi dresses, and resort flat-lay packs across 38 editorial looks.

CHAPTER 05 · THE APPAREL EXPANSION

Full Garments: Hoodies, Co-Ords & Flares

The newest drop breaks out of headwear and swim entirely. Full-zip cat-ear hoodies, wide-leg palazzo flares, joggers, off-shoulder sweaters, hooded vests, floor-length maxi dresses, arm warmers, leg warmers, fanny packs, totes and bucket hats — plus the Look-04 five-piece set in 12 colorways.

CHAPTER 06 · ATELIER COUTURE

The Patchwork Manifesto

The top of the house. Hand-crocheted avant-garde tailoring — origami-peaked shoulders, giant notched lapels, floor-puddling palazzo trousers — built in dense tapestry crochet with intarsia hand-lettering and surface-chain embroidery. Hundreds of hours per look. Made to order.
